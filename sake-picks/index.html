FROM PLACE COMES CHARACTER

Every bottle begins somewhere.

Behind every bottle is a place: its water, rice, climate, methods and the people who chose to make sake there. Sake Picks treats provenance as part of the drinking experience — because Japan is not one sake style, but many regional identities.

FAQ

Before you begin.

01What is Sake Picks?+

Sake Picks is a curated discovery platform for premium Japanese sake, built around taste, food pairing, brewery stories and real user interest.

02Can I buy sake through Sake Picks?+

Sake Picks is currently focused on discovery. Availability varies by bottle and market. Save what interests you and choose Notify for future availability updates.

03How are bottles selected?+

We look for distinctive taste, craftsmanship, regional identity, credible source information and a story worth understanding.

04What does “Want” mean?+

It saves a bottle to your list and gives Sake Picks a signal of real interest. That signal can help guide what we investigate and source next.

05Is Sake Picks only for sake experts?+

No. Sake Picks is intentionally designed so you can begin with food and flavor before learning technical classifications.
