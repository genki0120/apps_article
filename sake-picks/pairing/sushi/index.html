PAIRING GUIDE · 壱

Best Sake
with Sushi:
Choose by Fish,
Not by Price.

The best sake for sushi depends more on the fish, fat, texture and sauce than on the price tag. White fish usually benefits from clean, lightly aromatic sake; fatty tuna can carry more body and umami; shellfish likes brightness; spicy rolls often work better with softer fruit.

THE SIMPLE IDEA

The best sake for sushi
brings out the beauty of the fish.

“Sake for sushi” is too broad to be useful. A piece of sea bream, a cut of fatty tuna and a spicy roll ask for different things from the glass. Match weight, aroma, acidity and umami to the bite in front of you.

THREE PAIRING PRINCIPLES · 三つの考え方

A better sushi experience
starts with three simple ideas.

01 魚

Match the weight.

Delicate fish wants a lighter, cleaner sake. Rich fish can carry more body, texture and savory depth.

02 酸

Use acidity to reset richness.

Salmon and toro bring fat. A bright or structured finish keeps the pairing alive from bite to bite.

03 香

Let fruit soften heat and salt.

With spicy rolls or salty toppings, gentle fruit can round the edges without turning the pairing sweet.

04 · SHELLFISH

Brightness keeps salinity clear.

Scallop, oyster and shellfish often combine subtle sweetness with sea-like salinity. A precise, bright sake can lengthen those flavors without muting them.

Choose fruit over aggression.

Heat and rich sauce can make a very dry or alcoholic finish feel sharper. Softer fruit and a polished texture can calm the edges.

FAQ · よくある質問

Before your next sushi night.

Is Junmai Daiginjo always the best sake for sushi?＋

No. Grade alone does not tell you whether the sake matches the fish, fat, sauce or texture. Taste and balance matter more at the table.

Should sake with sushi always be chilled?＋

Many aromatic styles work well chilled, but richer Junmai, Kimoto and Yamahai styles can also work closer to room temperature or warm, depending on the sushi.

What sake works with fatty tuna?＋

Look for enough body, acidity or savory depth to stand beside the fat. A very light sake can disappear next to toro.

What is an easy starting point for sushi beginners?＋

A clean, lightly fruity style is accessible because it works with many delicate fish and does not require specialist vocabulary.
